Acervo dictionary storage probe

Open on the target phone and in the macos/ WKWebView host. Everything the spike assumes about what a client can read is measured here rather than inferred.

Serve this over https. OPFS and storage.estimate() both require a secure context, so opening it as a file:// URL or over plain http reports them missing on every device including desktop Chrome — which is a property of the page's origin, not of the device. python3 -m http.server on localhost also counts as secure.
