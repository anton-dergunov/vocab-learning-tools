## Two hard cases handled well without being asked twice

**`acordarse` — *no me acuerdo*.** Nothing to photograph, and an example sentence of three words. The
brief gave the missing memory a body: a hollow transparent silhouette leaving the man's open mouth
and breaking into fragments as it goes. Purely mental verb, and the picture is about *forgetting*
rather than about a worried man.

**`acostarse #2` — *se acostó con su compañero de trabajo*.** The "refuse narrowly" rule and the
"depict the situation, not the anatomy" line met the case they were written for. The brief is a
closed bedroom door in a dark hallway with a discarded dress and necktie outside it. Nothing is
refused, nothing is depicted, and the meaning is unmistakable.

`acosar` — *ella fue acosada* — went the same way: a woman under a streetlamp with three distorted
shadows looming on the brick behind her. A word the learner genuinely needs, and a picture of the
threat rather than the act.
